Our story

Why Yetisoft exists

We started Yetisoft because too many businesses in Nepal and beyond were stuck choosing between generic templates and expensive agencies that never quite understood the problem. We wanted to be the team that actually sits with a business, understands what it needs, and builds it properly.

Today that means working across web, mobile, AI, SaaS and IoT — not because we chase every trend, but because real projects usually need more than one of these working together.
