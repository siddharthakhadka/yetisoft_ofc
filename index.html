Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about working with Yetisoft.

How long does website development take?+

A simple website typically takes 2–3 weeks. Larger web applications or SaaS builds take longer depending on scope, usually 8–14 weeks.

How is pricing determined?+

Fixed-scope projects are quoted after a discovery call. Larger or ongoing work is scoped as a custom proposal based on your requirements.

Do you provide maintenance after launch?+

Yes. Every project includes a post-launch support window, with ongoing maintenance plans available afterward.

Do you handle hosting and domains?+

We can set up and manage hosting and domain configuration, or work with hosting you already have in place.

Do you offer SEO services?+

Yes — technical SEO is included in most website builds, with more in-depth SEO and digital solutions available separately.

Can you build custom software, not just websites?+

Yes. We build internal tools, mobile apps and custom systems designed around your specific workflow.

What kind of AI solutions do you build?+

From AI features added to existing products to custom models and automation pipelines — scoped to what's actually useful for your case.
